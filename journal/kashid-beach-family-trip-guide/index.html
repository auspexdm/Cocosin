Kashid Beach family trip guide: where to stay, eat & explore

Two easy days near Kashid Beach with kids, from the drive down to the sea fort.

Journal · Family travel · 20 November 2026

Kashid is one of the easiest beach trips from Mumbai for families: about 30 km beyond Alibaug and roughly 135 km from Mumbai on the Alibaug–Murud coastal road. Its white sand and long shoreline suit kids, and there's a sea fort and a forest sanctuary within half an hour. Here's how to plan two easy days.

Getting to Kashid

  • By road: about 135 km from Mumbai, through Alibaug, Revdanda and Korlai.
  • By ferry: catamaran from the Gateway of India to Mandwa jetty (about an hour), then about 50 km by cab.

For routes, maps and the ferry in detail, see How to Reach.

Where to stay with family

Coco's Inn Resort is in Barshiv village, just 2.6 km from Kashid Beach. It has sea view rooms, a swimming pool and Kokani dining, so kids can switch between beach and pool time without a long drive. Larger families can look at the duplex family sea view rooms.

Where to eat near Kashid Beach

Beach stalls near Kashid serve quick snacks and fresh fish. For a sit-down family meal, the restaurant at Coco's Inn serves multi-cuisine and Kokani food, including coastal seafood. See Dining.

A simple two-day family plan

  1. Day 1: Arrive by lunch, check in, pool time, then sunset on Kashid Beach.
  2. Day 2: Morning boat to Murud-Janjira Fort, lunch back at the resort, and an evening walk before heading home.

Tips for families

  • Swim only on calm stretches and where locals or lifeguards say it's safe. Stay out of the sea in the monsoon.
  • Go to the beach early in the morning or late in the afternoon, when the sand is cooler.
  • Weekends and holidays fill up fast, so book early.
